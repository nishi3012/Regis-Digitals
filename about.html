Mayank Chapanera is the Founder of Regis Digitals and Regis Healthcare. A pharmacy graduate (B.Pharma) who has worked in the healthcare industry throughout his career, he came to marketing from healthcare, not the other way round.

Regis Digitals is a boutique agency, and it stays that way on purpose. I don’t take on a client’s whole marketing on day one. We start small, with one clear piece of work, and see if we’re right for each other. Only then do I take on more.

Think of me as your chief marketing officer. You work with me from the first conversation to the last deliverable.

I came to this from healthcare. Before Regis Digitals, I co-founded Regis Healthcare, and agencies kept coming to me with pitches. None of them knew the industry: the doctors, the rules, or how patients actually decide. I got tired of it, so I built the agency I wished existed.

And I’ll tell you what not to do. If an ad, a campaign or a platform won’t work for your practice, you’ll hear it from me before you spend a rupee on it. Most of our clients stay for years, and that’s a large part of why.

The principles behind the work

A portfolio shows you what got built. This is how it feels to work together.

Three principles, each with a test that proves it. If one sounds unlike your last project, that’s the point.

Three principles

01

You work directly with me from the first call to launch day.

The person who scopes the problem stays responsible through to launch. Your feedback doesn’t pass through a team you never meet, so decisions stay clear and the work keeps moving.

One point of contact means fewer meetings, faster turns, and a straight line between what you meant and what ships.

02

The hundred small details get handled before anything feels off.

The spacing, empty states, and lines of copy all get the time they need. Most of the work is the part nobody sees, but it’s what makes a product feel considered.

That care goes in up front, rather than landing on you to catch what’s missing later.

03

The work earns its keep. That’s how we measure whether it was worth it.

The result matters as much as how it looks. If a requested direction won’t get you where you need to go, you’ll hear it while it’s still easy to change course.

Your users, conversion, and business goals get the same attention as the interface. The outcome is the point.
